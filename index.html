Selected manuscripts

Research that questions its assumptions.

Across causal learning, agents, and graphs, I design controls that reveal when external knowledge helps—and when a model should refuse it.

NeurIPS 2026 · Under review01

PRCD-MAP: Learning How Much to Trust Imperfect Priors in Causal Discovery

Learns edge-specific trust from data, using useful prior structure while safely reverting to data-only discovery when the prior is misleading.

Post-rebuttal reviews: 4/4/5
Xihang Shan, Da Zhou* PDF ↗GitHub ↗
AAAI 2027 · Under review02

Testing Before Trusting: Causal Skeptic Bandits under Unreliable Historical Evidence

Treats historical causal knowledge as a hypothesis to be tested online; diagnostic falsification and a causal veto keep misleading graphs from steering decisions.

Xihang Shan PDF ↗GitHub ↗
AAAI 2027 · Under review03

DeltaNAR: Learning What to Reuse and What to Recompute in Neural Algorithmic Reasoning

Separates state that remains valid after a graph update from state that must be recomputed, enabling incremental reasoning without unsafe copying.

Xihang Shan, Ye Luo* PDF ↗GitHub ↗
AAAI 2027 · Under review04

Failure-Learning Claims Need Controls: A Claim-Control Evaluation of Language Agents

Uses claim-matched controls to distinguish repair, transfer, target-solving, and executable validity from ordinary task success.

Xihang Shan PDF ↗GitHub ↗

Bounded Path Context: Visible Path History in LLM-Based KGQA
